Work he does that is not billable and not on the résumé: teaching, mentoring,
and explaining the job to people outside it.

**Deliberately vague about places and people.** The schools are described by

## Career days at rural Texas elementary schools

He has spoken at three or four elementary school career days over the years, in
small rural Texas districts, most recently in 2026.

The talk is cloud computing for children who have not met an engineer. What he
actually does, explained without jargon: the websites and games they use run on
computers in buildings somewhere else, somebody has to design how those
computers talk to each other, and that is the job. He brings hardware to pass
around, because an eight-year-old who has held a drive understands "the cloud is
someone else's computer" in a way no slide achieves.

Why it is worth recording: a career day is the hardest audience there is for a
technical explanation. There is no shared vocabulary to fall back on and no
patience for a wrong level of detail, so it is the same skill as a first
discovery call with a non-technical executive, practised on people who will tell
you immediately when you are boring. He has said the exercise improves how he
opens customer workshops.

## Enablement inside the team

The same instinct shows up at work, and most of it is already covered elsewhere
in this corpus: AllCloud's first handover-document template and discovery
questionnaire, the internal LZA configuration guide, the library of
AI-assistant skills encoding the team's SOW, estimate and design-guide
workflows, and the region-deployment runbook that ran past five thousand words.

He also reviews other architects' estimates and statements of work on request,
across a wide range of account executives and engineers, which is enablement
whether or not anyone calls it that.

## What this is not

He is not a conference speaker. He has not keynoted, has no talk circuit, and
no published papers. If someone asks whether he speaks at industry events, the
honest answer is no: he teaches children occasionally and colleagues
continuously.
