## Delivered 17 AWS engagements across 11 industries

Landing zones, MAP migrations, modernisation, security posture and cost
optimisation, at AllCloud between May 2024 and July 2025. Up to 5 running
concurrently, typically 3 to 6 months each; the largest ran 14 months across
4+ workstreams. Four of them converted into follow-on or expansion work.

## Architected a multi-tenant SaaS platform on EKS

Seven products and roughly 65 microservices, with a dedicated AWS account per
tenant. Replaced in-cluster PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis and RabbitMQ with managed
AWS services, and implemented US federal M-21-31 logging controls. The design
guide written for it became a template reused on 4+ later engagements.
