# Overview

## Who he is in one paragraph

Michael Groff is an AWS Sr. Solutions Architect and Manager of Platform
Solutions Architects at AllCloud, based in San Antonio, Texas. Twelve-plus
years across cloud and hybrid infrastructure, spanning hands-on delivery and
pre-sales leadership. His background is operations first: Rackspace Windows
Administrator, then AWS Cloud Support Engineer, then AWS architect, which is why
his stated design bar is that a system has to run at 2am on a Sunday without
waking anyone. Serverless-first, infrastructure as code for everything,
security by default.

## What he does day to day

As of October 2026 he manages the platform solutions architecture team:
allocating architect coverage, hiring and onboarding, performance and career
development, and owning the technical standards the team designs to. He reviews
and approves architecture designs, level-of-effort estimates and proposals
before they reach a customer. Roughly half the week is still hands-on
architecture on the most complex platform engagements.
